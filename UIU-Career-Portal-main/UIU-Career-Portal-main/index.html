Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Who can apply through UIU Career Portal?

All current UIU undergraduate/graduate students and registered alumni with a valid @uiu.ac.bd or verified student ID.

Q: How do companies get verified?

The UIU Career Services & Placement Office reviews company trade licenses and HR credentials before approving job postings.

Q: How can I track my internship approval?

Log in to the Student Portal and navigate to the 'Applications' tab to see real-time verification and supervisor approvals.
